# Overhead HP bars over the ring's kit fighters (src/coop/tny_hpbars.c): a Royal Rumble (every fighter gets one) and a
# 2 v 2 (the opponents keep their boss bars; no overhead bar on them). Debug-menu state of a DEBUG_MENU=1 build:
#   python tools/headless/st_test.py tools/headless/tny_hpbars.txt ROM build/<dir>/hl/menu.ss --out build/<dir>/hl
# Look at the shots: hb_rumble* show small bars over the Rumble fighters, hb_hit the struck ones shorter; hb_2v2 the
# two boss bars at the top and no small bars over those two.
