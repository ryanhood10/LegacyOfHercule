# Each is spawned as an opponent 48 px right of player 1 through COOP_CMD_SPAWN_RECORD (8), from the test enemy record

# the others: both run the melee boss AI set 21). He fights player 1 (Piccolo, a deep HP pool, punching back every
# 20 frames) for 10 s; every 20 frames 'peek e+0x48 2' prints the enemy's sprite record and current pose group

# warp (spawned on the same map after a long fight and a knock-out, the next body once showed in pieces for a second).
